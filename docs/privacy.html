Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

Tical doesn't collect any data. Your tickets are read on your device, and nothing you give Tical is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What Tical collects

Nothing. Tical has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It has no server, and it doesn't send your tickets, their details, or anything about you to the developer or to third parties.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. That's an Apple feature you control in Settings; Tical itself contains no analytics code.

How Tical reads your tickets

When you give Tical a screenshot, photo, or PDF, it reads it on your device: Apple's Vision framework finds the code and the text, and, where available, the Apple Intelligence model on your device works out the details. Tical doesn't use cloud processing, including Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

Tical keeps the ticket in memory while you review it and doesn't save it. When you share a ticket to Tical from another app, it's held in Tical's private storage on your device until Tical opens it, and deleted right after.

Photos, files, and the clipboard

Tical doesn't ask for access to your photo library, files, or clipboard. The photo picker, including the row of recent screenshots, and the file picker are system screens that give Tical only the items you pick. Tical reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste.

Calendar

Tical doesn't ask for access to your calendar and can't read your events. It fills in Calendar's own event editor, and iOS saves the event when you tap Add.

Apple Wallet passes

Tical builds and signs passes on your device. The passes it makes don't contact any server.

If you set up Wallet passes, Tical stores your Pass Type ID certificate and its private key in your device's keychain, marked to stay on this device: they don't sync to iCloud Keychain and can't be restored onto another device. You can remove them at any time in Tical under Settings › Wallet Passes › Remove Certificate.

When you import a certificate file that doesn't include Apple's intermediate certificate, iOS downloads that public certificate from the address listed in your certificate. No ticket or personal data is sent.

Settings

Tical stores two preferences on your device: the default event length and the start time for tickets that show only a date.

Links

Links to Apple's developer website open in your browser, where Apple's privacy policy applies.

Children

Tical doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
